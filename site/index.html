heartlock / heart.lock

heartlock

FROZEN BY MAKER
WHAT
A tiny package that gives any project a heart.lock: what it is, why it exists, and whom it is for, in one line each.
WHY
AI, tools and teams keep changing; the heart should not. The why stays frozen in the repo where every human and every AI can read it.
WHOM
Makers building with AI who do not want their projects to drift.
Heartbeat: last frozen 2026-11-25

Claim your listing

01

Add a heart.lock to your repo and freeze it.

02

Open a pull request against the heartlock repo adding your entry to site/beating.json.

03

Your entry is verified against the heart.lock in your repo before it merges.

04

Once merged and deployed, you are listed as frozen by the maker.

Claiming an example works the same way: the PR changes that entry's status to maker with the true freeze date.
