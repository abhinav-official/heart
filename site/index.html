heartlock

FROZEN BY MAKER
What
A tiny package that gives any project a heart.lock: what it is, why it exists, and whom it is for, in one line each.
Why
AI, tools and teams keep changing; the heart should not. The why stays frozen in the repo where every human and every AI can read it.
Whom
Makers building with AI who do not want their projects to drift.
